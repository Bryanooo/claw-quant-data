# Levels and Risk Research

Use repeated swing zones before formula pivots. Nearby pivot families are correlated, not
independent confirmation. Keep Fibonacci swing retracement separate from prior-period Fibonacci
pivots and exclude the disclosed live anchor. State risk windows, sample sizes, and benchmark.
Return zones and invalidation, not guaranteed targets or personalized position sizing.
