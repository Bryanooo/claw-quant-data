# Fundamental Research

Read `/readiness` and `/capabilities`, keep one `as_of`, then use
`/v1/research/stocks/{ts_code}/fundamentals`, `/valuation`, `/repurchase-progress`, and the stock
research pack as required. Inspect quality, provenance, gaps, and external-data requirements.

Analyze growth, accounting quality, valuation, capital allocation/shareholder return, and
falsifiers separately. Compare like-for-like fiscal periods and announcement dates. Distinguish
announced repurchase plans from executed cash return; never infer execution from price. Do not
manufacture fair value without forecast horizon, terminal growth, and discount-rate assumptions.
Show the few decision-driving trends as charts and state missing official evidence. Use the event
specialist for market response and the technical specialist for price/volume structure.
