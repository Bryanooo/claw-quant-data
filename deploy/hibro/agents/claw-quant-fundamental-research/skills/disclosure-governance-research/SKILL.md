# Disclosure and Governance Research

Inspect `external_data_needed` before external research. Prefer exchange, regulator, court,
issuer, and fund-manager sources. Record publication time, effective period, entity, document
type, URL, and supported claim. Keep official fact, media narrative, inference, and unresolved
hypothesis separate. Missing local rows mean unknown. If official evidence is inaccessible,
return the exact unresolved request and reduce confidence.
