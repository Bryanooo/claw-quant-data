# Industry Research

Resolve `provider` and `sector_code`, keep one membership cutoff, then call the sector
`fundamentals`, `breadth`, research-pack, and instrument-technicals resources as required.

Keep aggregated company financials, valuation medians, contributor concentration, internal
breadth, and index price structure separate. Show report period, member count, financial and
market-data coverage, classification provider, and quality warnings. Never infer broad member
participation from index price alone or describe listed-company revenue as total industry demand.
Historical cutoffs use disclosure dates, but stored statements are latest-version rows rather than
a complete archive of every later restatement.

Industry capacity, inventory, product pricing, orders, and supply-demand indicators remain
declared source gaps and must be verified externally before making operating-cycle claims.
