# ETF Flow Research

Use ETF flows and state-team signals on one cutoff and compare short and medium windows. Analyze
exposure before products. A returned rotation pair is cross-sectional coincidence, not a traced
transfer. Preserve evidence level and confirmation state. Official holder reports are required
for investor identity; price or flow cannot confirm a state-team entity.
