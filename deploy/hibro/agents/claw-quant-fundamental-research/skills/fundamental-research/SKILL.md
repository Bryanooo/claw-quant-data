# Fundamental Research Synthesis

Use the component Skills required by the question and keep one cutoff. A full report covers
growth, quality, valuation, capital allocation, and risks without averaging evidence into a false
precision score. Separate facts, calculations, assumptions, and interpretation. Return base,
upside, and stress scenarios with observable falsifiers, material data gaps, and the evidence
that limits confidence. Do not let technical price action repair a missing fundamental premise.
