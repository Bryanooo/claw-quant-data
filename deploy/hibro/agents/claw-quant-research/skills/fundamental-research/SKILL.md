# Fundamental Research

Use `/v1/research/stocks/{ts_code}/fundamentals`, `/valuation`, and when relevant
`/repurchase-progress`. Compare like-for-like fiscal periods and announcement dates. Distinguish
observed statements, derived ratios, assumptions, and interpretation. Do not manufacture fair
value without forecast horizon, terminal growth, and discount-rate assumptions. Show the few
decision-driving trends as charts and state falsifiers and missing official evidence.
