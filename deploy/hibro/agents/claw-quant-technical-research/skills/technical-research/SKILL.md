# Technical Research Synthesis

Load only the component Skills the question needs. For a full stock view, analyze price
structure, trend, momentum, volume confirmation, volatility/risk, levels, relative strength, and
wave alternatives in that order. End with primary and alternate scenarios plus price, volume,
and time invalidation. Do not average overlapping indicators into a false confidence score and
do not turn a structural candidate into advice.
