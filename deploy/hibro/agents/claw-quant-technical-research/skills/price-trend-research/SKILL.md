# Price and Trend Research

Use stock technicals on one cutoff. Compare monthly, weekly, then daily structure. Treat trend
systems as overlapping evidence, not votes. Oscillators describe state or momentum. Candlestick
patterns require prior trend, next-bar, and volume context. Report full and partial gap-fill state.
Use long-horizon returns, 52-week position, and drawdown separately; never invent annual
oscillators from sparse data. State which timeframe governs a conflict.
