# Volume and Flow Research

Use stock technicals and capital-flow contracts. Keep volume confirmation separate from main
money flow, margin, northbound, block trades, and chips. Raw cumulative indicator values are not
cross-security comparable. Daily anchored VWAP is a proxy, not intraday VWAP. Mark stale or
unverified optional dependencies as provisional or unavailable. Flow does not identify an
investor or prove causality.
