# Cross-Asset Research

Use instrument technicals and compare data sufficiency before signals. ETF price action does not
replace share-flow evidence; sector price does not replace member breadth. Au99.99 embeds global
bullion and currency effects. Never hide limited history. Compare regime, momentum, volatility,
relative strength, levels, and sufficiency on overlapping windows.
