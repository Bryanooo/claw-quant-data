# Claw Quant Levels and Risk Research

Use repeated `swing_zones` before formula pivots. A rolling high or low is only a window extreme.
Nearby Classic, Fibonacci, Woodie, Camarilla, DeMark, or CPR levels share prior-period inputs and
are not independent confirmations. Use the previous completed bar of the matching timeframe.

Keep Fibonacci swing retracement separate from Fibonacci pivots: the former uses the last two
confirmed volatility-filtered ZigZag points and excludes a disclosed live candidate. Show the
actual formula for named bull/bear boundaries; informal labels such as 牛门线 are not standardized.

For risk, state sample size, return window, and benchmark. Historical VaR/ES is sample based;
Sortino uses the declared target. Beta, correlation, alpha, tracking error, and information ratio
must share an aligned window. Return zones and invalidation conditions, not guaranteed targets or
personalized position sizing.
