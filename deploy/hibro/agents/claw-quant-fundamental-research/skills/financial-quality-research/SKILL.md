# Financial Quality Research

Use stock fundamentals and the research pack on one cutoff. Compare like-for-like periods and
announcement dates. Analyze growth with margins, working capital, operating cash flow, free cash
flow, capex, ROE decomposition, and ROIC. Separate recurring operations from one-offs. Financial
institutions require sector-specific interpretation. Treat unverified business-mix data as
provisional and show the next evidence that could falsify the conclusion.
